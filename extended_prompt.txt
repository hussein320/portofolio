This is a static website only with no backend, no forms with submission, no database, and no APIs. The experience must feel polished, modern, bright, elegant, and high-trust—closer to a premium data/AI consultant portfolio than a generic CV page. The site should present Hussein as an emerging but highly capable data professional with strong expertise across BI, data engineering, AI, geospatial fairness research, and visualization. The overall impression should be intellectually sharp, visually clean, and internationally credible, with a white/light aesthetic, strong typography, subtle motion, and an editorial layout that transforms resume content into a high-end digital identity.

1. Color Palette
Design this portfolio light-mode-first because the user explicitly requested bright/white colors, and because a clean light interface best communicates clarity, intelligence, and professionalism for a data science and BI profile.

Use a refined, airy palette with crisp neutrals and a restrained blue-cyan intelligence accent:
- Primary: #1D4ED8 — confident cobalt blue for primary CTAs, key links, highlighted metrics, and selected navigation states
- Secondary: #0F766E — sophisticated teal for supporting emphasis, data-related highlights, timeline accents, and badges
- Accent: #7C3AED — restrained violet for selective highlights, hover glows, and premium visual contrast in small doses
- Background main: #F8FAFC — soft off-white, never stark clinical white
- Surface/card: #FFFFFF — elevated card tone for layered sections and floating content blocks
- Surface alternate: #EEF4FF — cool pale blue-tinted surface for selected bands and stat strips
- Heading text: #0F172A — near-black slate for high contrast and authority
- Body text: #334155 — refined slate gray for readable long-form copy
- Muted text: #64748B — secondary descriptive text, captions, dates, locations
- Border/divider: #E2E8F0 — subtle separation for cards, grids, section boundaries
- Soft shadow tint: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08) for elegant card elevation

Interactive states:
- Primary hover: #1E40AF
- Primary active: #1E3A8A
- Secondary hover: #0F5F59
- Accent hover: #6D28D9
- Disabled: #CBD5E1 with reduced opacity
- Focus ring: #93C5FD for blue interactive elements, #C4B5FD for violet-accent interactive details

Gradient language:
- Hero gradient wash: linear blend from #FFFFFF through #F8FAFC to #EEF4FF for soft atmospheric depth
- Accent gradient: from #1D4ED8 to #7C3AED used sparingly on highlight text, small dividers, and CTA emphasis

Apply the 60-30-10 rule:
- 60% white/off-white neutrals
- 30% blue/teal intelligence tones
- 10% violet accent and gradient detail

Avoid loud saturation, harsh black, or startup-neon palettes. The website should feel bright but not sterile.

2. Typography & Visual Hierarchy
The typographic voice should communicate analytical precision with warmth and maturity. Use a modern geometric or neo-grotesk sans-serif for headings paired with a highly readable humanist or grotesk sans-serif for body text. The implementation agent should load fonts using next/font.

Typography direction:
- Headings: bold, clean, slightly condensed feel if possible, with tracking-tight and sharp line breaks
- Body: highly legible, slightly softer, comfortable for professional scanning
- Labels: uppercase, tracking-widest, small size, muted color for metadata and section intros

Recommended hierarchy:
- H1 hero name/title: text-5xl md:text-7xl lg:text-8xl, font-bold to font-black, tracking-tight, leading-none
- H2 section headings: text-3xl md:text-5xl, font-bold, tracking-tight
- H3 subsection/card titles: text-xl md:text-2xl, font-semibold
- Body text: text-base md:text-lg, leading-relaxed
- Meta labels and supporting text: text-sm md:text-base, text-muted color
- Timeline date/location text: text-sm, font-medium, uppercase or semi-uppercase treatment where useful

Weight distribution:
- font-black or font-extrabold for hero name and main statement
- font-bold for section titles and key numbers
- font-semibold for role titles, card headings, navigation emphasis
- font-medium for dates, labels, metadata
- font-normal for body copy

Special treatments:
- Use gradient text subtly on a small phrase in the hero, not the full headline
- Use uppercase tracking-widest for section intros such as EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION, PROJECTS
- Use oversized numeric stats as visual anchors
- Quotes or mission-like lines can use italic or lighter weight for contrast
